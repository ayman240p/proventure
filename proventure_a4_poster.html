Abstract

ProVenture is an innovative, hyper-local web directory engineered to bridge the operational gap between student micro-freelancers, campus gig providers, and local neighborhood clients. Conventional gig platforms enforce prohibitive commission structures (up to 20%) and complex escrow systems unsuited for low-cost, on-campus micro-tasks.

ProVenture addresses these frictions by coupling dynamic search indexing and price filtering with native WhatsApp Business URI deep linking (wa.me). This architecture eliminates intermediary transactional fees, empowers service providers with real-time availability toggles, and allows instant, fee-free client-provider negotiations.

Introduction

In academic environments and localized neighborhoods, micro-services such as peer calculus tutoring, PC/gadget reformatting, parcel dispatch running, and event graphic design represent vital supplemental income streams.

Problem Statement

Local micro-freelancers rely heavily on unstructured channels, presenting two major operational hurdles:

⚠️

Lack of Structure & Searchability

Ad-hoc WhatsApp groups and Facebook posts lack categorizations, keyword indexing, and live filters. Listings are rapidly buried under chat chatter, rendering services untraceable.

💸

Impracticality of Global Escrow Marketplaces

Platforms like Fiverr or Upwork levy steep commission fees (up to 20%), require complex international payouts, and focus on remote jobs rather than local physical micro-tasks.

4

Project Objectives

1

Responsive Directory & Filtering

To design and develop a responsive web directory that organizes local micro-services into structured categories with dynamic keyword and price-range filtering.

2

Zero-Fee "Click-to-Chat" Direct Integration

To integrate a direct communication mechanism utilizing the WhatsApp API URI scheme (wa.me), enabling seamless, zero-commission client-provider bookings.

3

Authenticated Provider Portal & Live Toggle

To build an authenticated provider portal allowing freelancers to perform full CRUD operations on service listings and toggle real-time availability status (Available/Busy).

⚡ Scope Focus: Feasibility-focused core delivery (Rating aggregation algorithm excluded to ensure practical viability).
